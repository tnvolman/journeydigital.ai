What We’re About

Three things you’ll notice fast

Jesus is the point.

Not politics, not a program, not us. We teach straight through Scripture and keep coming back to the same person.

Come as you are.

Nobody here has it figured out. Wear what you own, sit in the back, ask the question you’ve been carrying.

Doubt is welcome.

You will never be embarrassed for wondering out loud. Honest questions have always been safe around Jesus.

Meet Our Pastor

Hey, I’m Michael.

If you’re reading this on a Saturday night trying to decide where to go tomorrow — I get it. Walking into a new church is one of the more uncomfortable things a person can do.

So here’s what I can promise. Nobody will make you stand up. Nobody will ask you for money. You’ll hear the Bible taught plainly, you’ll probably laugh at least once, and you can leave without anybody chasing you to the parking lot.

Michael McClanahan · Lead Pastor
